# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, engineering leaders, clients, and technical collaborators evaluating Brandon Balcacer's experience in data analytics, data engineering, cloud systems, and applied AI. Visitors should be able to understand his current role, evidence of impact, project depth, and ways to contact him within a short scan.

## Product Purpose

Present a credible, current professional portfolio that turns resume claims into an easy-to-scan technical narrative. Success means the site accurately reflects Brandon's latest experience and projects, makes the strongest quantified outcomes prominent, and gives visitors direct paths to project evidence and contact details.

## Brand Personality

Technical, direct, and credible. The voice should feel like an experienced builder explaining real systems and measured outcomes, without inflated claims or marketing filler.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages, decorative AI imagery, glassmorphism, gradient text, indistinguishable card grids, and terminal styling used only as costume. Preserve the existing dark terminal identity while preventing it from becoming a novelty interface.

## Design Principles

- Lead with current work and verifiable outcomes.
- Make technical depth scannable without flattening it into buzzwords.
- Use the terminal language as structure and identity, not decoration.
- Prefer concise evidence over self-description.
- Keep contact and project destinations obvious and usable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and interaction behavior. Support reduced motion, visible keyboard focus, semantic heading order, descriptive link labels, and layouts that remain readable without horizontal scrolling from mobile through large desktop widths.
